# Dashboard

Use the dashboard to view, start and manage workouts for any day.

---

## View a day

The selected day appears at the top. You can:

- Use the **arrow buttons** to move between days.
- Tap the **date** to return to today.

---

## Start a workout

There are two ways to start:

1. **Begin Workout**
   Opens [Add Exercise to Workout](/pages/add-to-workout), where you can build a new workout.

2. **Copy Previous Workout**
   Opens [Workout History](/pages/workout-history). Select the **copy button** on the date you want to duplicate.

---

## Top buttons

The dashboard has four buttons at the top:

1. **Backup Button**

   - Back up your data manually or see when the last Google Drive backup occurred.
   - If automatic Google Drive backup is on, the icon shows a tick after a successful backup.
   - See [Backups & recovery](/pages/backups) for setup and restore instructions.

2. **Stats**

   - Open [Stats](/pages/stats/index) to see your progress over time.

3. **Workout History**

   - Open [Workout History](/pages/workout-history).

4. **Settings**

   - Open [Settings](/pages/user-profile) to manage backups and preferences.

---

## Share workouts

The dashboard can share workouts in two formats:

- **Share as Image** creates an image summary of your workout.
- **Share as Text** creates a plain-text version that you can copy or send in a message.
